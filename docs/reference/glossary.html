5.1 Row ablation. For each of the seven grade rows, isolate the days that passed every other row and measure what they did The extension guard was the volume suspect: 23,893 excluded days earning 1.43% against A+'s 0.83%, both spans, 527 tickers. The YTD penalty was richer but fragile. RSI and the trend condition were doing real work
5.2 Score-component ablation — the five ladders inside the score, which 5.1 never touched YTD is monotone increasing in forward return (1.16 → 2.44 → 5.33 → 11.76) and >150% is not tail-driven. MACD and MA flip sign between eras, both significant. The zero-ablation method was confounded and said so
6A Profit targets — sell a portion at 2R, 3R, 4R, or floor the stop at breakeven No arm adopted. The breakeven floor was inert — armed 435 times, changed 19 exits. 58.9% of touched trades improved and the dollars still lost, because a few runners pay for everything
6B Sizing × exits. The two components 5B deliberately froze NOT RUN. This is the open question, and Build 7 pointed at it
7 Carry replay — remove the extension guard at system level, crossed with risk-parity sizing The guard stays (inside the noise band). Risk-parity sizing LOSES −4.41 CAGR points, CI excluding zero. And the book is capacity-bound: removing the guard lifted A+ days 1.81× but produced fewer trades, because ceiling denials more than doubled
grade
history		 Backfilled from git, 17 trading days, plus forward persistence Live median A+ spell 1 day among closed spells. 23 distinct names touched A+ in 17 days — A+ is not scarce, it is fleeting. A+ falls to C ten times for every twice it falls to B
ribbon
probe		 Standalone study of moving-average ribbon dip-buying, 23 long-history ETFs. Never touched SignalAgent Closed as an entry edge. CAGR delta negative for all 23 symbols in both variants while cutting drawdown massively — the fourth independent confirmation that trend structure costs return and buys drawdown protection

Scorer versions

versionwhat it doesstatus
v1 YTD anchored on whatever the frame held — after early July that meant a rolling six-month return labelled YTD. And a penalty: above 100% YTD the component turned negative, down to −7 FROZEN. Kept callable so Layer A, 5B, 5.1, 6A and 7 still reproduce. But it still has six live production call sites — the UUP and XLE breaker inputs
v2 Real calendar YTD anchored on the prior year's last close, on both paths. The penalty is gone, replaced by a flat cap at +12 above 20% Production since 2026-08-09 on the score and grade paths. NOT yet live on the universe path — that artifact is rebuilt weekly and predates the cutover
The trap this creates: signals.json is stamped scorer_version: v2 while carrying near-miss rows with YTD point values v2 cannot emit, because those rows come from the older universe artifact. The stamp does not distinguish them.
And a rule for any new study: declare which scorer it runs on. Everything committed ran on v1. A v2 study is not comparable to any of it and cannot reuse the pinned frames.

Rules, units and other shorthand

termmeaning
R28 Position limits — ≤20% of capital and ≤3 names per group, under a regime-scaled total ceiling. Measured at market value, not cost basis. Reporting-hard: it cannot block a broker order and does not pretend to
R24Profit targets — the gap 6A tested and left unfilled
R11Exit discipline — what governs a position once held
1R One unit of initial risk: entry price minus the initial stop. Fixed at entry, never re-based. Every study reports expectancy per R
fwd-20d Forward 20-trading-day return, measured open-to-open — from the next day's open to the open 20 sessions later. Never from the signal-day close, which nobody could have traded at
ex-top-5% The mean with the best 5% of days removed. The test for whether a result is broad or moonshot-driven
random-K
band		 The null. Run the same machinery with randomly chosen names instead of selected ones, 50 seeds, and see whether the real arm escapes the resulting range. In 5B, nothing did
the extension
guard		 Row 2 — a name may be at most 1.8×ATR above its 20-day average at entry. Because the stop is that same average, this is also a cap on risk per share
weeks_in_
universe		 How many consecutive weekly rotations a group has survived. A group at 1 is one rotation from taking its holdings with it
thesis-failed What a holding becomes when its group leaves the universe. A different exit path from the stop — and one the stop cannot protect against
pin A test with three requirements: drive the real entry point, assert the invariant rather than the observed output, and be demonstrated to fail against the reintroduced bug
fan-out Adversarial review — independent agents attacking a finished build before it stages. Has changed a verdict five times
