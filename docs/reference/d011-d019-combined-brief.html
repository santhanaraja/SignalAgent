5 · Recommendation

Two rulings, one sequencing constraint, and one thing I would not touch.

  1. D-019 → suspend breadth as a throttle (19-B), explicitly interim. The measure has two documented misreads in three weeks, in opposite directions, and it is currently the sole constraint on the book. VIX and HY continue unchanged, so you keep two of three throttles and the entire chassis. The suspension expires when the replacement is swept — export $SPXA50R, run the frontier, re-rule. This is the one change where the evidence is specific, the mechanism is understood, and the cost of being wrong is bounded by the chassis itself.
  2. D-011 → hold the gate, but fix the window (11-G). The gate threshold is genuinely unresolved — the evidence supports neither loosening nor tightening, and every option except holding requires acting on a difference 5B could not measure. But the flicker is not a threshold question, it is a timing question, and it is measurable: an A+ that prints Thursday is unactionable by Friday's open. Extending actionability to N sessions changes nothing about which names qualify and everything about whether you can act on them. It also has a clean harness test — replay S1 with N ∈ {1, 3, 5} and see whether entry count rises without expectancy falling.
  3. Do not touch the stop, the ceiling ladder, or the close-basis law. Every study this project has run — Gauge B, Layer A, 5B, the addendum, both ribbon probes — points at the same conclusion from a different angle: the machinery is the edge. Arm drawdown bands never overlapped SPY's under any seed. That is the one seed-independent positive in the entire evidence base.
What this ships, concretely. A regime that upgrades to Trending on the next two clean closes (ceiling 90%, grade advisory again) · an A+ that stays actionable for several sessions instead of one · a dated, expiring suspension with a named replacement study · and no change whatsoever to stops, sizing, or the ladder. On Monday it means GEN becomes enterable when its guard line catches up, and a Thursday A+ is still a Monday trade.
What I am NOT recommending, and why it matters. I am not recommending you buy anything specific. Three weeks of live operation have produced one entry and my two published rankings were both wrong on outcome (PFG over NOW; the structural-quality read on GEN). The rulings above are about removing an unproven constraint and a measurable timing defect — not about my judgment of names, which has no track record worth weighting.

What this brief does not settle

Suspending a throttle is a loosening, and I am recommending it on three weeks of live observation. That is a small sample and the honest counterweight to everything above. The mitigations: the chassis, the ceiling ladder and the stops all remain, VIX and HY continue firing, and the suspension carries a written expiry.

11-G is invented, not measured. No study in the packet tested an actionability window. That is why it is gated behind a harness replay before it ships, rather than ruled straight into production.

The gate threshold remains genuinely unresolved. Nothing here rules on A+-vs-B. 5B could not distinguish them and this brief does not pretend otherwise — it addresses timing, which is measurable, and leaves the threshold where the evidence left it.

My name-level judgment has a negative live record. Two published rankings, both wrong on outcome, both wrong via structural-quality reasoning that Layer A had already flagged as unsupported. Weight the systemic arguments here; discount the discretionary ones.
